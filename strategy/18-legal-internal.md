## B. Breach Response Plan template

Law: DPA 2019 s.43 and regs 37 and 38. Notify the Data Commissioner without delay and within 72 hours of becoming aware if there is a real risk of harm. A processor tells the controller within 48 hours. Tell affected people in writing within a reasonably practical period unless encryption protects the data. Record every breach [V per strategy/17].

1. People: incident lead [NAME AND PHONE], backup [NAME], advocate [NAME AND PHONE], Privacy Contact [PRIVACY CONTACT NAME].
2. Hour 0: stop the leak (revoke keys, rotate passwords, disable the route, remove WhatsApp Web sessions), keep logs and screenshots, write down the time you became aware.
3. By hour 24: what data, how many people, how it happened, whether it was encrypted, likely harm to them, whether children's data is involved.
4. By hour 72: notify the Commissioner if the risk is real. Include date and circumstances, chronology, numbers, harm, steps taken and a contact person (reg 38). Channel: [CONFIRM CURRENT ODPC BREACH CHANNEL] (start at https://www.odpc.go.ke).
5. Tell the people in plain words: what happened, what data, what to do, who to contact. Send by WhatsApp or email. Say nothing that is not yet known.
6. Tell processors and, where needed, the courier, the accountant and the bank or M-Pesa provider.
7. Record: facts, effects, remedy, notification times, decisions and who made them (s.43(8)).
8. After: fix the cause, update the DPIA and the risk list, retrain, review at 14 days.

Exercises: walk through the plan once before launch (DP-18). Keep this page and a phone list offline.
